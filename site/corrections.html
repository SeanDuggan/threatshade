//Corrections and right of reply

Last updated: 13 November 2026

We want every claim on Threatshade to be accurate, fair and backed by a public source. If something is wrong, out of date or missing context, please tell us. We would rather fix a mistake than leave it up.

Report an error

Email info@threatshade.com with:

  • the organisation's name and the claim you think is wrong (you can copy the text);
  • what is wrong with it;
  • a link to, or copy of, the record that shows the correct position, if you have one.

Each polluter file on the scoreboard also has a "Report an error in this entry" link that starts the email for you.

If your organisation is listed

You can ask us to correct a factual error, add context (for example, work done since a conviction or listing), or publish a short reply. Email info@threatshade.com from an address at your organisation.

  • Replies: we will publish a reply of up to 200 words on your entry, unchanged, as long as it is about the entry and is not defamatory or offensive. We may add a short note if a reply disputes a court or EPA record.
  • New information: if a conviction was overturned, a listing ended or a figure was revised, send us the record and we will update the entry and its score.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge every request within 7 days.
  • We aim to decide within 30 days, and tell you what we decided and why.
  • If a request shows that a claim is likely to be seriously wrong, we take that claim down while we check it.
  • We correct anything we got wrong, and record the change in the log below.
  • We don't remove accurate, sourced information just because an organisation disagrees with our scoring. Our method, and the reasons for every score, are on the main page.

We are volunteers, so these are aims rather than guarantees, but we take every request seriously.
