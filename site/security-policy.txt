Threatshade vulnerability disclosure policy
===========================================

Threatshade is an independent, volunteer-run project that tracks pollution
in Ireland using public records. It is not a business and has no budget.
We still want to hear about security problems and will treat reports
seriously.

How to report
-------------
Email security@threatshade.com with:
  - what you found and where (URL, file or component)
  - steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
  - the impact you think it has

Please do not include personal data belonging to anyone else.

What to expect
--------------
We are volunteers, so these are aims rather than guarantees:
  - We will acknowledge your report within 7 days.
  - We will keep you updated and tell you when it is fixed.
  - Please give us 90 days to fix the problem before you publish details.
    We can agree a different date with you.

Rewards
-------
We cannot offer payment or a bug bounty. If you would like, we will thank
you by name (or handle) when the fix is released.

In scope
--------
  - The website at threatshade.com and its subdomains
  - The Threatshade source code and data pipeline

Out of scope
------------
  - Disagreements about a listed organisation's data or score. Please send
    these as factual corrections, with a link to the source.
  - Third-party services we use (for example Cloudflare, GitHub, EPA
    websites). Report those to the provider.
  - Missing best-practice headers or settings with no demonstrated impact
  - Denial of service, load testing, spam or social engineering
  - Results from automated scanners without a working proof of concept

Safe harbour
------------
If you act in good faith, follow this policy, avoid harming users, data or
availability, and only test as much as you need to show the problem, we will
not take legal action against you or ask anyone else to.
