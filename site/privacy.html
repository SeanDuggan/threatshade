//Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: this site uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics, and it has no accounts, forms or comments. While you browse it, your browser only talks to threatshade.com.

What we don't collect

  • No cookies and nothing stored in your browser (no local storage).
  • No analytics or tracking: no Google Analytics, no advertising or social media pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party requests. The page, its code, fonts, map, data and icons are all served from threatshade.com. Nothing is loaded from Google, social networks or anyone else.
  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The filters, sliders and Sitka spruce overlay all work inside your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and basic request details (such as the page requested and your browser type) in order to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare handles that data under its own privacy policy. We can see overall traffic numbers in Cloudflare's dashboard, but we don't use them to identify or follow anyone.

Links to other websites

Every score links to its sources, such as the EPA, the courts and newspapers. If you follow one of those links you leave this site, and that website's own privacy policy applies. We send no information about you when you do, beyond what your browser shares by default (we limit the referrer to "threatshade.com").

If you email us

If you write to security@threatshade.com, we use your email address and message only to reply and deal with what you raised, and we delete them when they are no longer needed. We never share them or add you to a mailing list.

People named on the site

The scoreboard lists organisations, not private individuals. If you think the site contains personal information about you, or you want to correct something, email us and we will look at it promptly.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Who we are

Threatshade is an independent, volunteer-run group tracking pollution in Ireland from public records. Questions about this page: security@threatshade.com.
